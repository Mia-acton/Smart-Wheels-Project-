About Smart Wheels

Driven by Intelligence, Powered by Innovation

🚗 Why Smart Wheels?

Road safety is more than speed — it's about awareness, prevention, and responsibility. Smart Wheels was created to enhance driver awareness and control using smart sensor technology. Our goal is to make every journey secure and efficient.

⚙️ How It Works

Using sensors such as MQ3 (Alcohol), IR Flame Sensor, MQ2 (Gas), and Speed Sensors, our Robocar continuously monitors the environment. Real-time data is displayed using the ThingSpeak platform, allowing drivers to respond quickly to hazards.

👨‍💻 Meet the Founder

Mia Acton, a robotics enthusiast from Galway, Ireland, founded Smart Wheels with a vision to merge safety and automation. Mia believes that the future of transportation lies in vehicles that think with us — not just for us.
