Sensor Technologies

Advanced technology ensures smarter, safer performance for your Robocar.

MQ2 Gas Sensor 💨

Monitors and detects harmful gases such as smoke, methane, and carbon monoxide, ensuring a safe operating environment.

MQ3 Alcohol Sensor 🍺

Activates the Robocar only when no alcohol is detected upon startup, preventing impaired operation.

IR Flame Sensor 🔥

Detects fire using infrared waves and triggers alerts to prevent damage or danger in real time.

IR Tachometer ⏱️

Records speed precisely using infrared reflections, allowing for accurate performance analysis and control.
